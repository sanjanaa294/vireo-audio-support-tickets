# Memo — Tier 1 Support Coaching Priorities

**To:** Priya Raman  
**Subject:** Tier 1 support performance and coaching priorities

## Executive summary

I built a support performance dashboard to identify Tier 1 agents who may benefit from targeted coaching. The analysis focuses on three operational measures: customer satisfaction (CSAT), median handle time, and first-response SLA breaches.

For Jan–Jun 2026, the strict Tier 1 population covered **2,840 tickets**. The first-response SLA breach rate was **9.2%**, representing **260 breaches** and approximately **₹91,000 in SLA credits** at ₹350 per breach.

The proposed improvement target is a **20% relative reduction in SLA breaches**, taking the rate from 9.2% to approximately 7.3%. At Vireo's current volume of approximately 650 tickets per week, this represents roughly **₹54,000 in avoided SLA credits per quarter**.

## What the dashboard identifies

The dashboard produces a Bottom-10 coaching shortlist using:

- CSAT performance — 50% of the risk score
- Median handle time — 30%
- First-response SLA breach rate — 20%

Agents are compared within their own Tier 1 team rather than across structurally different queues. The analysis also requires at least 30 tickets and 30 CSAT responses before an agent enters the shortlist.

A manager can select an individual flagged agent and review the underlying evidence, including operational ticket themes and sample agent notes.

## Recommended action

The Bottom 10 should be treated as a **coaching shortlist, not a final performance or bonus decision**.

For each flagged agent, review a small sample of low-CSAT or SLA-breach tickets first. The objective is to determine whether the issue is actually individual performance or instead reflects response speed, resolution complexity, queue/process constraints, or the type of work being handled.

This is particularly important because some support queues have structurally different work patterns.

## Measurement

After coaching, the same dashboard can be rerun on a subsequent period to measure whether:

1. First-response SLA breaches decline.
2. CSAT improves.
3. Handle time moves closer to the relevant team peer range.

The primary business KPI should be the reduction in SLA breaches because Vireo's policy assigns a **₹350 store credit for each first-response SLA breach**.

## Bottom line

The immediate opportunity is not simply to identify the lowest-scoring people. It is to use the data to identify **where targeted coaching is most likely to improve customer experience and reduce avoidable SLA-credit costs**, while keeping operational context in the decision.
